Ambi generated from conversation context · DEEP = CT Dept. of Energy & Environmental Protection · OPM = Office of Policy & Management · DPH = CT Dept. of Public Health · Critical path note: DPH data-sharing MOU with YSPH typically takes 6–10 weeks -- likely the longest lead-time item
Sources backing each pathway node
DEEP -- policy track CT Executive Order 21-3 (Dec 2021) directs DEEP to lead climate adaptation planning and establishes the CT Climate Adaptation Office within DEEP's authority. DEEP administers the Climate Resilience Fund and serves as the primary policy approver for cool corridor and urban heat programs. portal.ct.gov -- EO 21-3
OPM -- funding track The CT Office of Policy and Management administers the Urban Act Grant Program (CGS 4-66c), which funds capital infrastructure projects including community resilience and cooling programs in designated urban centers such as New Haven. OPM receives and reviews funding requests and routes them to implementing agencies. portal.ct.gov -- Urban Act The DCRF (DEEP Climate Resilience Fund) has explicitly funded cool corridor planning in Connecticut -- Groundwork Bridgeport received $249,816 in the 2023 inaugural round specifically to identify cool corridors in urban tracts. DCRF Round 1 press release
DPH -- data auth track CT DPH controls the Vital Records Registry, birth certificate data, and the CT-LLoVE longitudinal linkage dataset (mothers linked to birth outcomes 2010-2020). Access to maternal outcomes data for public-facing use requires a Data Use Agreement executed with the CT Office of Health Strategy and DPH's Human Investigations Committee (IRB). The process involves a multi-step application, Data Release Committee review, HIPAA compliance verification, and signed agreement before data release. CT OHS data request process

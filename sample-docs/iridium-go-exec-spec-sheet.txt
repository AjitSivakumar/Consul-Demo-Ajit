IRIDIUM GO! exec — PRODUCT SPECIFICATION SHEET
================================================

Overview
--------
The Iridium GO! exec is a portable satellite hotspot that creates a personal Wi-Fi zone
connecting up to 5 smartphones or tablets simultaneously via the Iridium satellite network.
Unlike the Extreme (voice-first), the GO! exec is optimized for data-rich applications:
photo upload, weather map download, social media posting, and email with attachments.

Key Specifications
------------------
- Weight: 300 g (10.6 oz)
- Dimensions: 120 × 82 × 24 mm
- Operating temperature: -20°C to +55°C
- Ingress protection: IP68 (submersible to 1m for 30 min)
- MIL-STD 810H certified
- Battery: Lithium-polymer, 3,500mAh
- Hotspot active time: Up to 7 hours
- Standby: Up to 16 hours

Connectivity
------------
- Wi-Fi hotspot: 802.11b/g/n, range ~30m
- Simultaneous connections: Up to 5 devices
- Data throughput: Up to 22 kbps (compressed), ~168 kbps (burst, Certus 100 mode)
- Photo upload: Compressed JPEG (~200KB) in ~45 seconds
- SOS: Built-in emergency button with GPS
- Tracking: Configurable position reports every 3-60 minutes

Companion App
-------------
- Available for iOS and Android
- Push-to-talk voice via VoIP over satellite
- Messaging (SMS bridge, email)
- Photo compression and upload queue
- Weather overlay with GRIB file download
- Track sharing with live map

Use Case: Expedition Media & Logistics
--------------------------------------
The GO! exec is the preferred device when the expedition team needs to:
  1. Upload daily progress photos for sponsors / social media
  2. Download weather forecast maps and GRIB data
  3. Send/receive email with attachments (route plans, medical reports)
  4. Run tracking for base camp to monitor team position

Deployment History — Polar Expeditions
--------------------------------------
- Used by Preet Chandi 2022 South Pole solo crossing for daily Instagram photo uploads
- National Geographic polar photography teams: standard kit 2023-2025
- Expeditions using GO! for media reported 3.2x higher sponsor engagement vs voice-only
- Average daily data transfer per expedition: 1.4 MB (photos + weather)

PREET CHANDI — SOUTH POLE 2026 EXPEDITION BRIEF
=================================================

Expedition Overview
-------------------
- Expedition: Solo, unsupported crossing of Antarctica to the South Pole
- Explorer: Captain Preet Chandi MBE ("Polar Preet")
- Departure: November 2026 from Hercules Inlet, Antarctica
- Target: Geographic South Pole (~1,130 km journey)
- Duration: Estimated 40-50 days
- Support: Unsupported — all supplies pulled on pulk (sled), no resupply drops

Previous Record
---------------
- January 2022: Preet became the first woman of colour to complete a solo expedition
  to the South Pole, covering 1,130 km in 40 days
- Used Iridium Extreme 9575 as primary comms device — zero reported call failures
- Daily check-in protocol: 2 voice calls + 4 SBD position reports per day
- Media engagement: Instagram posts via satellite generated 2.3M impressions over expedition

2026 Expedition — Enhanced Requirements
-----------------------------------------
Compared to 2022, the 2026 expedition has expanded communications needs:

1. VOICE (same as 2022)
   - Daily check-in with base camp: 2× per day, ~10 min each
   - Emergency SOS capability with GPS position
   - Weather briefing calls with meteorologist

2. DATA (NEW requirement for 2026)
   - Daily photo upload for sponsors and social media (~3-5 photos/day)
   - Weather map download (GRIB files, ~50KB each)
   - Email correspondence with expedition manager
   - Sponsor-mandated daily blog post (~500 words + 1 photo)
   - Live GPS tracking shared publicly via GTC TrackOps

3. REDUNDANCY (safety requirement)
   - Antarctic safety protocols recommend dual-comm capability
   - If primary device fails, backup must support voice for emergency
   - Historical data: 8.3% of Antarctic solo expeditions report at least one device failure
   - Dual-device teams: 0% communication blackouts
   - Single-device teams: 3.1% experienced >24hr communication blackout

Weight & Power Budget
---------------------
- Total pulk weight budget: 90 kg (all gear, food, fuel)
- Communications weight allocation: max 800g including batteries
  - Iridium Extreme: 247g + spare battery 80g = 327g
  - Iridium GO! exec: 300g + spare battery 95g = 395g
  - Total dual-device: 722g — within budget
  - Solar panel (shared): 320g — optional, depends on conditions

- Power consumption per day:
  - Extreme: ~20 min voice + SBD = ~250mAh/day → battery lasts ~9 days
  - GO! exec: ~30 min hotspot active = ~500mAh/day → battery lasts ~7 days
  - Cold weather factor: multiply consumption by 1.4x at -30°C
  - Recommended: 3 spare batteries total, charge via solar when possible

Sponsor Requirements

- Equipment sponsor (Iridium, if deal closes): Logo placement on pulk, 2 pre-departure press events

Decision Criteria (from GTC)
-----------------------------
1. Proven reliability in polar conditions (Iridium: ✓ 2022 track record)
2. Photo upload capability for sponsor obligations (GO! exec: ✓)
3. Total cost within expedition comms budget ($5,000-$8,000)
4. Weight within 800g allocation
5. Ease of use with gloves / in tent conditions
6. Emergency SOS with rapid response time

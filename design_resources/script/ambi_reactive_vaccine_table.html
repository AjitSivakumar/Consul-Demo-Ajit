⚠ Roseland — clinic closure flag
The 52% unvaccinated figure for Roseland (60628) is in flux. A primary community clinic closed in October 2024, mid-data-collection. The elevated rate likely reflects a supply disruption rather than a stable signal of underlying demand. Do not use this figure as a pilot anchor without flagging the context to the board.

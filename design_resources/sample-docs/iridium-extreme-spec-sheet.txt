IRIDIUM EXTREME 9575 — PRODUCT SPECIFICATION SHEET
=====================================================

Overview
--------
The Iridium Extreme 9575 is a ruggedized satellite phone designed for operation in the
most demanding environments on Earth. It connects via the Iridium LOW Earth Orbit (LEO)
satellite constellation, providing pole-to-pole coverage including polar regions, open
ocean, and remote terrain where terrestrial networks are unavailable.

Key Specifications
------------------
- Weight: 247 g (8.7 oz) with battery
- Dimensions: 140 × 60 × 27 mm
- Operating temperature: -20°C to +55°C
- Ingress protection: IP65 (dust-tight, water jet resistant)
- MIL-STD 810F certified (humidity, salt fog, vibration, shock, rain, dust)
- Battery: Lithium-ion, 3.7V 2300mAh
- Talk time: Up to 6 hours
- Standby time: Up to 30 hours
- SOS button: Dedicated programmable emergency button with GPS coordinates

Voice & Data
------------
- Circuit-switched voice: 2.4 kbps vocoder
- SMS: Up to 160 characters, store-and-forward capable
- Short Burst Data (SBD): Up to 1,960 bytes MO / 1,890 bytes MT
- GPS: Integrated GPS receiver, position accuracy ±10m
- Emergency SOS: One-touch alert with lat/long to GEOS emergency response center

Satellite Network
-----------------
- Constellation: 66 active LEO satellites at 780 km altitude
- Coverage: 100% of Earth's surface including both poles
- Latency: ~300-600 ms round-trip
- Handoff: Seamless inter-satellite crosslinks, no ground station dependency for routing
- Uptime: 99.9% network availability (historical 2020-2025)

Deployment History — Polar Expeditions
--------------------------------------
- Used by 94% of Antarctic expeditions 2020-2025 as primary voice device
- British Antarctic Survey: standard issue since 2018
- Preet Chandi 2022 solo crossing: Extreme carried as primary comms, zero call drops reported
- Average mission comms uptime (dual-device teams): 99.2%
- Average mission comms uptime (single-device teams): 91.7%

Roseland (60628) reported unvaccinated rate — do not anchor on this figure. The Roseland Primary Care Clinic closed October 2024, removing residents' primary vaccination access point mid-collection. Comparable low-income zip codes with intact access show 38–44%. The true underlying rate is likely in that range.
⚠ Risk — if you lead with Roseland
The clinic closure is public record. A board member or funder will find it. Being caught without the context damages credibility on the other three zip codes — which are solid.
